### Stay Safe Online: A Prevention Guide

* **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Always use 2FA on your college email, LinkedIn, and social media accounts to add an extra layer of security.

* **Verify UPI IDs Before Paying:** Always double-check the name of the recipient in your UPI app (like GPay or PhonePe) before entering your PIN to avoid payment fraud.

* **Never Share Your UPI PIN:** Remember that a PIN is only required to send money, never to receive it. If someone asks for a PIN to "credit" your account, it is a scam.

* **Use Strong, Unique Passwords:** Avoid using easily guessable passwords like your name or birthdate; use a combination of symbols, numbers, and letters.

* **Identify Phishing Attempts:** Be wary of emails or texts claiming you have won a prize or that your bank account is blocked. Never click on suspicious links.

* **Secure Your Social Media:** Regularly review your "Tagged" posts and privacy settings to ensure your personal life isn't visible to strangers.

* **Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Banking:** Do not log into your bank or student portal using open, public Wi-Fi networks in cafes or stations.

* **Think Before You Post:** Once something is online, it is permanent. Avoid posting content that could be used for identity theft or cyberbullying.

### How to Report Cybercrime in India
If you or a peer are a victim of a digital crime, use these official channels immediately:

* **National Cyber Crime Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/) 

* **National Helpline Number:** 1930 (Available 24/7)
