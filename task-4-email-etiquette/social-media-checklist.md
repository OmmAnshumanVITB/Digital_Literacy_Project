### Social Media Etiquette Guide for College Students
As a Student Digital Ambassador, I recommend the following guidelines for maintaining a responsible and professional online presence:

**The Do's**
* **Do** use privacy settings to control who sees your personal posts and information.
* **Do** think critically before sharing or interacting with news to avoid spreading misinformation.
* **Do** credit original creators when sharing photos, articles, or digital work.
* **Do** use a professional profile picture and bio on platforms like LinkedIn.
* **Do** engage in constructive discussions that add value to your professional community.

**The Don'ts**
* **Don't** post offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content that could harm your future career.
* **Don't** engage in cyberbullying, harassment, or heated public arguments with others.
* **Don't** share sensitive personal details like your home address, phone number, or OTPs.
* **Don't** post confidential information about your university, professors, or peers.
* **Don't** use unprofessional language or excessive slang in public academic forums.
